## Evidence-informed design choices

[Ryan, Rigby & Przybylski (2006)](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2006_RyanRigbyPrzybylski_MandE.pdf)
connect autonomy, competence and relatedness with enjoyment and future play. Here,
players choose difficulty/timer, receive understandable distance feedback, and
share equal-rule challenges without an account. These are design inferences,
not measured engagement results for this app.

[Loewenstein (1994)](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/sds/docs/loewenstein/PsychofCuriosity.pdf)
describes curiosity arising from a perceived knowledge gap. A mystery image,
optional hint and clear city reveal apply that idea. There are no forced daily
check-ins, paid retries, streak-loss penalties or invented popularity claims.
